Hello my love, 🤍

I know main tumhare jaisa acha nahi likh paunga na, kuch utni achi tarhan explain kar paunga… but still. 🥹

So first of all, I'm really sorry ke main tumhe 23 October par wish nahi kar paya aur bhool gaya. 🥺🤍 But agla 23 October main bohot special banane ki koshish karunga, InshaAllah… apni jaan ke liye. 🤍🌸

And main shayad alfazon mein bata nahi sakta, but tumne mujhe mere har ache-bure time mein jitna support kara hai, mujhe motivation di hai… uski wajah se tumne mere dil mein apne liye bohot zyada jagah bana li hai, aur apne aap ko mere liye bohot special bana liya hai. 🫶🏻🤍

Mujhe aaj bhi woh din yaad hai jab tumne mujhe pehli dafa bataya tha ke tum mujhe pasand karti ho. Sach mein, us din mujhe khushi se na neend aayi, na yakeen hua. 🥹❤️

Aur us din ke baad se tumne har din ko mere liye special aur calm bana diya hai. 🌸

Jaise zindagi bohot halki ho gayi hai… like calm water. 🤍

Dil mein sukoon hai tumhare hone se. Aur agar kabhi bechain hota bhi hoon, toh tum mujhe apne haathon mein lekar bilkul bachay ki tarhan khamosh kar deti ho. 🥹🤍

Tumhare saamne main ro bhi leta hoon, kyunke pata hai ke tum mujhe chup karaogi, meri baat sunogi… sabki tarhan ignore nahi karogi. 🫂

And that's why I love youuu more than anyone. ❤️
